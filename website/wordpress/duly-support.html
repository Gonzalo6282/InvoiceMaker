Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Duly works as soon as you open it. Sign in with Apple is optional and only links your subscription across your devices.

What can I do without paying?

Create, edit and preview as many invoices, estimates and receipts as you like, save clients and items, back up to iCloud and export your data. Sending, printing and saving PDFs need Duly Premium.

Where are my invoices stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud. We never upload them to our servers and we can't see them. Keep iCloud turned on so a new iPhone or iPad gets everything back.

My invoices didn't come back on a new phone

Check that you're signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud has free space. In Duly, Settings > iCloud backup shows when the last backup and restore happened, and the reason if one failed.

How do my customers pay me?

Add your bank details and payment links (PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App or any payment link) in Settings > Payment details. They appear on every invoice with a QR code. Payments go straight to you.

How do I restore my subscription?

Settings > Account > Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Payments are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. For a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I export everything?

Settings > Export everything makes a ZIP with every PDF and a CSV of your documents, clients and items.

How do I delete my account?

Settings > Account > Delete account. Your invoices stay on your device and in your iCloud; delete the app to remove them.
