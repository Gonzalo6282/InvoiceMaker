From the estimate to the receipt, on one phone

Everything happens where the work happens. No laptop, no evening paperwork.

  1. Quote the job on site

    Write an estimate in front of the customer, let them sign it on your iPhone, and turn it into an invoice with one tap when the work is done.

  2. Fill in the invoice in a minute

    Pick a saved client and saved items, and Duly works out the tax, discounts and total. Everything saves as you type.

  3. Make it look like your business

    Your logo, your brand colour, your signature and seven templates. See the PDF exactly as your customer will.

  4. Get paid without chasing

    PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, a payment link and your bank details go on every invoice, with a QR code your customer can scan.

  5. Send a receipt as proof

    Mark the invoice paid and make a receipt from it in one tap, so your customer has it for their records.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Duly works as soon as you open it. You can sign in with Apple later if you want your subscription on another iPhone or iPad.

Where are my invoices stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud. We never upload them to our servers, and we can't see them.

How do my customers pay me?

Add your payment links and bank details once in Settings. They appear on every invoice with a QR code. The money goes straight to you; Duly takes no cut.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep every invoice, estimate and receipt, and you can still open and export them. Only sending, printing and saving PDFs stop until you subscribe again.

How do I cancel?

Payments are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
